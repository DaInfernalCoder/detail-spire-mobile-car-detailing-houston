# Detail Spire design direction

## Subject, audience, and job

Mobile detailing for Houston drivers who want a methodical rise from base cleaning to final visible detail. The page should show that progression and lead to calling or booking online once contact details are confirmed.

## Visual system

- Spire violet — `#25223A`
- Deep iris — `#413B64`
- Stone mist — `#E6E4EE`
- Detail white — `#FBFAFD`
- Summit amber — `#E0A43A`
- Display: Archivo Black
- Body: Manrope
- Utility: IBM Plex Mono

## Layout thesis

The editorial frame turns its circular inspection cue into a vertical rise from broad cleaning toward precise finishing.

## Signature

A faceted spire climbs through two inspection rings, encoding progress without making unsupported transformation claims.

## Critique

The first version resembled outdoor adventure branding. The revision grounds the spire inside automotive inspection rings and keeps scenery out.
